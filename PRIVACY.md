# Privacy Policy for LeetRepeat

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

LeetRepeat is a Chrome extension that helps users review LeetCode problems through spaced repetition. This Privacy Policy explains how the extension handles information.

## 1. Information We Store

LeetRepeat stores the following information locally in your browser:

- LeetCode problem titles
- Problem URLs
- Problem solve dates
- Revision schedules and completion status

This information is stored using Chrome's local extension storage and is used to maintain your revision dashboard.

## 2. How We Use Information

The stored information is used exclusively to:

- Track problems you have solved on LeetCode
- Schedule revision reminders at 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day intervals
- Display revision progress and status in the extension dashboard
- Allow you to mark problems as revised or remove them from tracking

## 3. Data Storage and Sharing

LeetRepeat stores its tracking information locally in your browser.

**LeetRepeat does not transmit, sell, or share this information with the developer or third parties.** The extension does not operate a backend server for collecting user tracking data.

Your information remains in your browser unless you remove it or uninstall the extension.

## 4. Website Access

LeetRepeat accesses LeetCode problem pages (`https://leetcode.com/problems/*`) to identify accepted submissions and obtain problem titles and URLs for tracking.

The extension does not intentionally access unrelated websites.

LeetRepeat is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LeetCode.

## 5. Notifications

LeetRepeat uses Chrome notifications and alarms to remind users when tracked problems are due for revision.

These reminders are generated locally by the extension.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Tracked problem information remains in Chrome storage until you remove individual problems, clear the extension's stored data, or uninstall the extension.

You can remove tracked problems directly from the LeetRepeat dashboard.

## 7. Security

LeetRepeat relies on Chrome's extension storage and permission model to store and manage tracking information. Users should keep their browser and extensions up to date.

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published at this page.
